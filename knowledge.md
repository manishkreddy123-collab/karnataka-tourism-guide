# Karnataka Tourism Guide: curated knowledge base

This guide is maintained separately from the Python code and loaded into the system prompt for each chat. It is a starting set of general destination information, not a live directory or booking service. Use the linked official sources to verify details before travelling.

## Places and broad trip themes

- **Hampi:** Explore the remains of the Vijayanagara-era city, including temple complexes, the Vitthala complex and its stone chariot, royal and sacred areas, and the surrounding boulder landscape. Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage property.
- **Pattadakal, Badami, Aihole, Belur, and Halebidu:** Heritage options for travellers interested in historic temple architecture and sculpture. Use official site information to plan access and route logistics.
- **Mysuru:** A city option for palace and cultural heritage visits. Check the Mysuru Palace official site for current visitor information.
- **Western Ghats and hill-country routes:** Coorg/Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Sakleshpur, Kemmanagundi, and Agumbe are broad nature and hill-country options. Local weather and road conditions can affect outdoor plans.
- **Waterfalls and nature:** Jog Falls and other waterfall areas are seasonal nature options. Conditions, access, and safety depend on recent rainfall and local notices.
- **Coastal Karnataka:** Mangaluru, Udupi, Gokarna, Malpe, and Kaup offer coastal, cultural, and food experiences. Sea and beach conditions vary; follow local safety notices and restrictions.
- **Wildlife:** Bandipur and Nagarhole are wildlife-tourism options. Entry, safari availability, permitted routes, and rules are controlled by relevant authorities and operators; do not promise sightings or availability.
- **Bengaluru:** A city base with urban culture, food, arts, and transport connections. Avoid asserting live traffic, service, or event information.

These destination groupings are ideas for discussion, not a verified itinerary. Do not invent distances, journey times, transit services, road conditions, or exact route order. Ask the traveller for trip length and interests if needed.

## Broad seasonal and practical planning

- Karnataka includes coast, plains, cities, forests, and higher-elevation areas. Weather and suitable activities vary by region and year.
- As a broad planning heuristic, the cooler, drier part of the year is often more comfortable for outdoor sightseeing in many areas. This is not a guarantee or a destination-specific forecast.
- Monsoon rainfall can affect hill roads, trails, waterfalls, wildlife visits, beaches, and transport. Do not describe a site as safe or open based on season alone.
- For exact travel dates, recommend checking the India Meteorological Department forecast and official local advisories close to departure. This chatbot has no live forecast or alert feed.
- Carry drinking water, sun protection, suitable footwear, and weather-appropriate clothing as relevant to the destination. Respect local customs, protected areas, wildlife rules, and posted signs.
- **Timings, closures, entry rules, ticket prices, and fees may change.** Verify latest details with the official attraction, government department, or operator before travel. Do not guess or give unverified current prices or schedules.

## Regional food examples

Karnataka food varies across regions. These examples are a starting point, not a complete list or a guarantee that a dish is available at a particular restaurant:

- Statewide or widely known examples: bisi bele bath, Maddur vada, Mysuru masala dosa, ragi mudde, and holige.
- Coastal examples: seafood dishes and Kundapura koli saaru.
- Do not claim a restaurant is open, safe for an allergy, or serving a dish without verified current information. Travellers with allergies should confirm ingredients and cross-contamination directly with the restaurant.
