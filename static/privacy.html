Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Please do not share sensitive information. This service is public and does not require an account.

Information you provide

When you send a message, the service processes its text and a randomly generated session identifier. Chat messages and recent conversation context are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. Do not include names, student or USN numbers, phone numbers, exact addresses, credentials, or other sensitive information.

Storage and service providers

The service stores chat messages, session identifiers, timestamps, response times, token counts when provided, and feedback ratings in a SQLite database on its hosting server. The hosting provider may also process technical request and service logs. OpenAI processes submitted messages under its applicable API terms and privacy practices. The API key is kept on the server and is not sent to your browser.

Retention and control

There is no account or user-facing chat deletion control. Starting a new conversation does not delete previous server-side records. Database availability and retention may be affected by hosting configuration, deployments, or service restarts. Do not use this service to store personal or confidential information.

Cookies and location

The chat uses browser session storage to retain a conversation identifier. It does not request browser location. The guide does not provide live weather, maps, transport, booking, or government-alert data.

Contact

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact the operator through the channel where you accessed this service.
