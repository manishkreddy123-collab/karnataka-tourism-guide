KARNATAKA TOURISM GUIDE · DEMO

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Demo notice: This is not a reviewed production privacy policy. Do not enter personal or confidential information.

Information not to share

Do not enter names, USNs or student numbers, phone numbers, exact home addresses, identity documents, or other sensitive information. Messages are stored as submitted.

How messages are handled

Anyone with the public service URL can use the chat without an account. Messages and recent conversation history are sent to the configured OpenAI API. The API key stays on the server. SQLite stores messages, timestamps, response times, returned token counts, and submitted feedback.

Storage and retention

Starting a new chat does not delete old server-side logs. This sample has no automatic deletion schedule or user deletion controls. Do not submit confidential information.

Live travel data

The guide does not access live location, weather, maps, transport, bookings, or government alerts. Hosting infrastructure and the AI provider may process technical data under their terms.

Before production use

A real operator must document data flows, establish retention controls, secure access and storage, and obtain privacy, security, and legal review.
